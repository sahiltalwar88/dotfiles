# Spaced hyphens in Sahil's copy are intentional

When Sahil writes copy for the pwep-consulting-website pages, he uses a spaced
hyphen as a dash ("real results - not just a story"), and he does so on
purpose. Reviewing his Observatory text, I recommended converting two of them to
em dashes to match other places on the site; he declined: "i prefer dashes, even
though i know they're grammatically wrong."

So in a copy review, do not list spaced hyphens as a problem, and never "fix"
them to em dashes or en dashes when editing his text. He already knows the
typographic rule and has chosen against it. Em dashes that are already in the
codebase (for example the "— Sahil Talwar" attribution) can be left as they
are; the preference is about what he writes, not a mandate to rewrite existing
punctuation.

A related but narrower note from the same review: he kept "amongst" over
"among" in the Russell strip because it matches the voice of the essay being
paraphrased. That was a choice for that passage rather than a site-wide rule,
so treat British-leaning word choices in his copy as possibly deliberate and
ask before changing them.

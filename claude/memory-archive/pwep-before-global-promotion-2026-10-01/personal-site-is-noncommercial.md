# /personal is non-commercial; pwep consulting is a separate site

When licences come up for third-party assets in the pwep world, NonCommercial terms such
as CC BY-NC are **acceptable on /personal**. Sahil settled this on 2026-09-15, when a CC
BY-NC Cloud Strife model was flagged because the site promotes a consulting business:

> "/personal is not selling anything, we're in the clear - pwep consulting is a different
> website, and while one links to the other, they have very distinct purposes"

So do not raise NonCommercial as a blocker again for assets that live only on /personal.
It is still a blocker for anything on the consulting site, and an asset used on both needs
a licence that allows commercial use.

Attribution is still required. CC BY and CC BY-NC both need the credit to reach the people
who see the work, so every attributed asset goes in `CREDITS.md` and on the public
attributions page listed as open work in `PLAN.md`.

This settles only the licence question. Rights in the characters themselves, such as
Nintendo's Pikachu or Square Enix's Cloud, are a separate question that Sahil has deferred.

# "s1" is the first screenshot attached to that message

When Sahil attaches images and refers to "s1", "s2", "s3" and so on, he means
those attachments numbered in the order he sent them. He stated the convention
directly when he answered a set of six test captures:

> "as for your screenshot requests: 1: s1, 2: s2, and so on"

It applies to a single attached image too. In an earlier message he wrote "the
road and some other terrain in the background randomly pop in and show up
through the fog before fading back into the fog - s1, let's fix that", where
"s1" pointed at the one screenshot on that message. I read it as "step 1" and
treated it as a priority marker, which was wrong — he was naming the evidence,
not the ordering.

So read `s<N>` as a pointer to an image, and go and open that image before
answering. If a reply needs to distinguish several of his captures, number them
back to him the same way rather than inventing labels of your own.

# The human figures in the world are Sahil, and he is an Indian man

Every human figure across the pwep world — the cyclist on the Road, the person in
the Observatory, the figure on the mountain, the one in the Archive, and the
rider inside every vehicle — is Sahil himself. He asked for them to "look like an
indian man (to a reasonable extent obviously, they're still pretty far away)"
rather than the generic pale stick figures they were.

So when building or revising any figure in this world, give it his skin tone,
hair and build, at whatever level of detail the viewing distance justifies. The
qualifier matters: he is not asking for a portrait, and most of these figures are
tens of units from the lens where only silhouette, proportion and colour read at
all. Getting the tone and the hair right at that distance is the whole job.

This is the same principle as the standing rule that the vehicles are one man's
continuous journey rather than separate props placed per zone — the world is
autobiographical, and the figure in it is him. A new figure added in a future
zone inherits this without needing to be told again.

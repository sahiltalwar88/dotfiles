When the user refers to something I don't recognise or don't fully understand (a repo, a person, a file, a term, a past decision), or says something ambiguous, I ask them what they mean rather than guessing and acting on the guess. The user said to always default to asking in this situation.

Why: acting on a wrong guess wastes their time and can change the wrong thing. This applies to discrepancies too, for example when their instructions describe a file, setting, or behaviour that doesn't match what I find: I report the mismatch and ask before choosing an interpretation.

When I ask the user questions that each carry a recommended answer (they often ask for questions in that format before work starts), a reply of just "rec" to a question means "go with your recommendation" for that question. Treat it as an explicit approval of the recommended option exactly as I stated it — no need to re-ask or confirm. This keeps their answers short when they agree.
